# Core Concepts

Here’s an overview of the essential ideas behind Jovyan AI.

## Foundational Models (LLMs)

Jovyan AI is powered by advanced language models trained on massive datasets. Models such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, and Mistral form the foundation, providing deep language understanding and the ability to generate high-quality code and text.

## AI Agents

AI agents in Jovyan are autonomous assistants capable of interpreting complex tasks, breaking them into manageable steps, and executing with a variety of tools. These agents can reason about your project, make informed decisions, and carry out actions to advance your objectives.

## Provider

Jovyan AI offers a built-in API provider so you can seamlessly access the latest leading coding models—no API key management needed. This provider connects you with frontier models, including OpenAI (GPT-4), Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), Mistral, Grok, and more.

## Context Management

The quality and relevance of LLM output depends heavily on context. Jovyan’s agents automatically gather and maintain pertinent details from your codebase, data, and past conversations. Power users can also manually manage and refine this context for more precise and efficient results.

## Task

A **Task** is a piece of work you assign to Jovyan AI. Tasks can be as simple as “explain this function” or as complex as “build a data pipeline and visualize the results.” Jovyan AI decomposes tasks into actionable steps, executing them methodically. All actions and messages within a Task are kept together in that Task’s context.

## Modes

**Modes** are specialized AI agent types, each tailored for certain tasks. In Jovyan AI, you can select modes to best fit your workflow:
- **Code:** Write code, execute commands, analyze data, and more within your project.
- **Architect:** Explore and design repository structure and architecture.
- **Ask:** Search, query, and understand your project contents.
- **Debug:** Diagnose and resolve errors or issues.
- **Orchestrator:** Coordinate multiple tasks or agents across modes.
